How it works.

01

Find the experts

Quoted experts, their roles and affiliations are pulled from the article. Spokespeople and officials are set aside.

02

Confirm who they are

ORCID must return exactly one match for the name and affiliation, or you pick the right record. No match, no search.

03

Find disclosed ties

Exa searches research papers and Tavily searches the web. A tie counts only if a page names both the person and the organization.

04

Check the money

CMS Open Payments confirms payments from the same company to a physician with that name, shown with location so you can judge.

Limits. Conflict Check shows connections disclosed somewhere else (papers, bios, payment records) that an article didn't mention. It can't find ties that were never disclosed, and “no confirmed connections” is a normal result.

Every quote is checked against the page it came from. Open Payments matches by name and location, not employer.
